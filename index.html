Packs

Your progress is saved to your Discord account, so it follows you to any device. Amber (in progress) ticks save straight away. Green (complete) ticks are collected in your Submissions tab, where you can add footage links and notes and send them all for approval together. They only appear on your profile and the Stats Viewer once approved.

Tip: press and hold a tier’s coloured title bar for two seconds to tick every level in it. Do it again to untick them.

Import from this browser

This browser still holds progress from the old local profiles. Pick one to bring it into your account. Complete levels are submitted for approval and in-progress ones are saved as amber.

Display

Shows all of a list’s tiers together on one page instead of splitting them across Novice, Intermediate, Advanced and so on.

Switches back to the site’s original look: square tier boxes and plainer buttons, menus and panels.

Experimental

Adds special effects to the tracker: a tier’s banner glows when you tick a level in it complete, keeps a soft glow once you reach a rank, and glows brighter when the whole tier is complete.
